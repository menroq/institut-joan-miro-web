Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It only uses the technical cookies that some services strictly need to work, so we do not need to ask for your consent when you visit.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. This policy also covers similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage.

2. Cookies used on this website

ServiceOwnerTypePurposeWhere it is activated
reCAPTCHAGoogleTechnical (security), third-partyTo tell people apart from automated programs and prevent spam through the formOnly on the Contact page
Google MapsGoogleThird-party, non-technicalTo show a map of the school's locationOnly if you click “Show map” on the Contact page

The school's own website does not set cookies on any other page.

By clicking “Show map”, you agree to Google loading the map and its cookies. If you do not click it, no Google Maps cookies are set.

To learn how Google uses this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and the reCAPTCHA terms.

3. Links to social media and other websites

The Instagram, YouTube and Clickedu icons are plain links: they do not set cookies while you browse this website. When you click them and leave for those services, their own cookie policies apply.

4. How to manage or delete cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

If you block reCAPTCHA cookies, you may not be able to submit the contact form. In that case, you can contact the school by email or by phone.

5. Changes to this policy

If the website adds new services that set non-technical cookies, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before they are set. For more information on how we process data, see the Privacy policy.
