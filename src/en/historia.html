History

Institut JOAN MIRÓ has 30 years of experience as a secondary and sixth-form education centre. The average experience of our teaching staff is around 25 years, with full dedication to teaching, research and personal attention to students.

Teaching teams made up of staff from the different subject areas monitor students both academically and personally, coordinated by each group's form tutor.

The most important goal of Institut Joan Miró is academic success; that is why support classes are offered on site and school trips and extracurricular activities are scheduled according to academic needs.

Our challenge is to achieve the highest quality in teaching. Practically all students obtain the Compulsory Secondary Education Certificate. A large majority complete their Sixth Form studies and go on to further education at university. We place special emphasis on guidance so that every student at Institut Joan Miró leaves the school with a solid academic and personal foundation.
